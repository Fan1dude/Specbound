Community Guidelines

Specbound is a place to document and share real technology projects. These guidelines explain what's expected of everyone who posts, comments, or otherwise participates here.

Last updated August 11, 2026

In short

  • Specbound exists to help builders document and learn from real projects.
  • Document your work clearly and honestly, and give feedback that helps others improve.
  • Harassment, spam, plagiarism, and low-effort engagement-bait aren't tolerated.
  • Use a project's or comment's Report button to flag content that breaks these guidelines.

Why these guidelines exist

Specbound works because builders trust what they see here — real projects, honestly documented, from people who actually built them. These guidelines exist to protect that, not to police tone or personality. Most builders never think about them because they're already doing the right thing.

Sharing and giving feedback

Document your own work clearly and honestly — what you built, what you used, what worked, and what didn't. When you comment on someone else's project, focus on the work: what could be improved, what you'd try differently, or what you liked. There's a real difference between "the cable routing on this could be cleaner" and criticizing the person who built it. The first helps. The second doesn't belong here.

Respectful conduct

Harassment, hate speech, and threats aren't allowed, whether they're aimed at another builder or at people generally. This includes targeting someone for their race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or another protected characteristic. It also includes encouraging or threatening violence. These rules apply in comments, profiles, project descriptions, and anywhere else on Specbound.

Content boundaries

Keep content appropriate for a general audience that includes teenagers. Do not post sexual content or content primarily intended to shock or disturb. Do not share someone else's personal information — such as their address, phone number, workplace, or other private details — without permission, and do not use Specbound to track down or expose someone.

Spam, scams, and impersonation

Do not use comments, profiles, or projects to spam, run scams, or push unrelated promotions. Do not pretend to be someone else, including another builder, a moderator, or Specbound itself.

Project authenticity and other people's work

Post your own work. If a project builds on someone else's design, code, writing, or other material, say so and credit them. Do not present someone else's project as your own, and do not upload content you do not have the right to share.

Dangerous or illegal activity

Do not use Specbound to document or promote illegal activity or content whose primary purpose is causing harm to people, property, or systems. Projects involving electronics, power tools, 3D printing, and similar work can carry real risks. Builders share what worked for them, but nothing on Specbound is professional electrical, safety, legal, or engineering advice, and Specbound does not verify or endorse the safety of any project. Use your own judgment and follow the safety practices appropriate to what you are building.

How reporting works

If you see a project or comment that breaks these guidelines, use its Report button and describe what happened. Reports are recorded for moderation review. Submitting a report does not guarantee a particular outcome or response, and reports should not be used simply because you disagree with someone's opinion or criticism.

Enforcement

Specbound may remove content or restrict, suspend, or remove accounts when necessary. The response depends on the seriousness of the behavior, whether it has happened before, and the surrounding context. Not every report results in action because reported content does not always break these guidelines.

Questions or disagreements

If you have a question about these guidelines or believe an enforcement decision was made in error, use the Feedback link in the site footer. Specbound does not currently provide a formal appeals process, and submitting feedback does not guarantee a response or a different outcome.
