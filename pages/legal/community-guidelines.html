Community Guidelines

This page states the real structure Specbound's Community Guidelines will follow. The wording below is placeholder — final language has not been written or approved yet.

Why we have guidelines

Specbound exists to help builders document and learn from real projects. These guidelines exist to keep that possible — not to police tone or personality.

What's encouraged

Clear, honest documentation of your own work. Constructive feedback that helps another builder improve. Sharing what you learned, including what didn't work.

What's not tolerated

Harassment, spam, plagiarized project content, and low-effort posting aimed at engagement rather than sharing real work.

How reports work

Any builder can report a project, comment, or profile they believe violates these guidelines. A moderator reviews every report — see the Report control available on projects and comments.

Enforcement

A moderator or staff member may take action on content that violates these guidelines. The exact range of actions isn't finalized yet.
