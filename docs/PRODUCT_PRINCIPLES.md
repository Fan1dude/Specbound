# Specbound Product Principles

Status: Authoritative. Approved 2026-07-28.

This is the belief system behind `SCOPE.md`'s explicit in/out list — the "why" for every line in that document's Explicitly Out of Scope section.

---

## Our Mission

Help creators preserve the story behind everything they build.

---

## What We Believe

Every build has a story.

Progress matters more than perfection.

Documentation creates knowledge.

The journey is more valuable than the destination.

Builders become better by sharing what they learn.

---

## We Build Features That...

✓ Encourage documentation

✓ Help creators continue projects

✓ Teach future builders

✓ Organize technical knowledge

✓ Celebrate progress

---

## We Avoid Features That...

✗ Encourage low-effort posting

✗ Reward popularity over quality

✗ Distract creators from building

✗ Exist only because other social platforms have them

---

## Every Feature Must Answer

Does this help someone preserve their engineering journey?

If not,

it probably doesn't belong in Specbound.

---

## Success

Success is not measured by likes.

Success is measured by knowledge preserved.
